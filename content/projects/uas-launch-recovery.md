## Overview
I am interning at [Seasats](https://www.seasats.com/), where I work on autonomous launch and recovery of unmanned aerial systems from unmanned surface vehicles.

The problem is a moving-platform one: the deck the aircraft has to leave from and return to is a small boat that pitches, rolls, and heaves with the sea state, and neither vehicle has a human in the loop. Launch and recovery therefore have to be handled as a coordinated behavior between the two vehicles rather than as an aircraft-only maneuver.

More on the approach and results to come.
